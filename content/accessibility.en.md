# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated:** July 14, 2026

The Emoryx website (`https://emoryx.erezults.com`) is operated by
**Erez Cohen**, doing business as **erezults**, based in Israel
("**the Developer**", "**we**"). We consider digital accessibility a
core value and work to make sure everyone, including people with
disabilities, can use this site independently, safely, and
comfortably.

---

## 1. Conformance level

This site has been designed to conform to level **AA**, in line with
the Israeli Standard **IS 5568** (guidelines for accessibility of web
content) — based on **WCAG 2.1** — and with the Equal Rights for
Persons with Disabilities Regulations (Service Accessibility
Adjustments), 2013.

Accessibility measures implemented on this site include:

- **Full keyboard navigation** — every link, button, and interactive
  element (including the FAQ accordion and the language switcher) can
  be operated and navigated using the Tab key alone, with a clear,
  visible focus outline.
- A **"Skip to content" link** at the top of every page, letting
  keyboard and screen-reader users bypass the repeated top navigation
  and jump straight to the main content.
- **Meaningful alt text** on every informative image, so screen
  readers can describe them to blind and low-vision users.
- **Compliant color contrast** — text against its background meets a
  contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text, per the standard's
  requirement.
- An **on-site accessibility widget** offering larger text, a
  high-contrast mode, and a "stop animations" toggle — in addition to
  automatic support for the system-level "reduce motion" preference
  (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
- **Semantic structure** — correct use of headings, `main`/`nav`/
  `footer` landmarks, and ARIA attributes (e.g. `aria-expanded`,
  `aria-pressed`, `aria-label`) on interactive components.
- **Full bidirectional support** — the site works correctly in both
  Hebrew (RTL) and English (LTR), including reading direction, text
  alignment, and keyboard navigation.

## 2. Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully
adapted — for example, content originating from external sources
(such as Apple's App Store listing) that is outside our control. We
continue to review and improve the site's accessibility on an
ongoing basis.
